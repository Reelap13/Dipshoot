The agent-based configuration uses the following numerical targets. The source-half structure target is
\[
    N_{struct}^{source}
    =
    \frac{\operatorname{round}(W \cdot H \cdot 0.4)}{2}
    =
    \frac{\operatorname{round}(15 \cdot 28 \cdot 0.4)}{2}
    =
    84 .
\]
The source-half cover target is
\[
    N_{cover}^{source}
    =
    \frac{\operatorname{round}(W \cdot H \cdot 0.12)}{2}
    =
    25 .
\]

During clustered container placement, high containers are selected with probability
\[
    P(ContainerHigh)=0.15,
\]
while the remaining containers are low containers. Bridge count is derived from the number of already occupied structural cells:
\[
    targetBridges =
    clamp(\operatorname{round}(occupiedCells \cdot 0.25), 2, 6).
\]
The ladder target is based on the amount of structure in the source half:
\[
    targetLadders = \max(2, structureCells / 8).
\]

Cover generation uses three main probabilities: full cover is selected with probability \(0.45\), elevated top cover is attempted with probability \(0.45\), and random cover rotation is attempted with probability \(0.22\).

The fitness report produced by this generator is used as validation and diagnostics. It describes route cost, clear-zone violations, line of sight, density, cover placement, and elevated access, but it does not act as an optimiser and does not select between multiple candidate layouts.
