The shared navigation model evaluates two types of routes. The weighted route cost is computed over both ground and elevated movement levels:
\[
    C(path) = \sum_{i \in path} (1 + e_i),
\]
where \(e_i=0\) for a normal cell, \(e_i=2\) for a cell affected by partial cover, and \(e_i=4\) for a cell affected by full cover. The maximum accepted weighted path cost is \(55\).

Ground-only connectivity is evaluated separately using an unweighted breadth-first search over ground-level cells:
\[
    C_{ground}(path) = |path|-1 .
\]
This check ensures that the capture point is reachable without relying exclusively on elevated traversal.

Cover also affects movement direction. A cover object stores a blocking direction vector \(d\). Movement from one cell to another with movement vector \(m\) is blocked when
\[
    m = d \quad \text{or} \quad m = -d .
\]

Line of sight is checked by tracing grid cells between a spawn point and the capture point. The line is considered blocked if any intermediate cell is occupied by a ground-blocking structure. Direct line of sight between a spawn point and the capture point is penalised by the validation and scoring systems.

The two teams' weighted route costs are compared using an allowed difference of \(8\):
\[
    |C_A - C_B| \le 8 .
\]
